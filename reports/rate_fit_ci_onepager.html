Highlights

  • Scrapyards: crusher and shredder spikes give a load factor of about 0.10. Because demand dominates the bill, 66% are on a wrong rate; GSDT/SDTR bill only on-peak or 3-6 PM demand, and moving crushing to before noon adds more.
  • Juice processors (Tropicana-style): run 24x7 at a load factor of about 0.60, with the citrus harvest peak Dec-May and a low Jun-Sep season. HLFT and SDTR are the levers, and 15% are mis-rated.
  • Medium GSD and large commercial: AC-driven afternoon peaks mean most are right on GSD-1/GSLD-1. The exceptions are sites with low 3-6 PM demand, where SDTR Option B wins.
