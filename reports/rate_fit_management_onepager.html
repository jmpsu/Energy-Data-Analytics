What the data says

  • Churches / houses of worship: 35K of 40K (86%) are missing TOU or SDTR; Sunday and Wednesday-evening peaks fall off-peak, so they are worth $81.3M/yr (about 2,357 dollars per church).
  • Residential RTR-1: 171K of 208K current RTR-1 customers (82%) would pay less on plain RS-1 ($28.3M/yr). Afternoon AC puts about 33% of kWh on-peak, above the roughly 30% break-even.
  • Residential should-be-TOU rate: 10.1% of EV owners and 12.5% of solar homes, vs 1.2% of other homes. These are the natural targets for RTR-1 marketing.
  • Schools: 4,812 should add SDTR ($26.5M/yr), because summer break removes their 3-6 PM Jun-Sep demand. Convenience stores: 27K have a material saving ($40.3M/yr), mostly high-load-factor GS-1 sites near 25 kW that would pay less on GSD-1.
  • Northwest (ex-Gulf) division: 27K customers on a wrong rate, $21.6M/yr. Mix: residential $35.6M vs C&I $333.5M. With a modest 10% on-peak load shift, the upside grows by a further $447.2M/yr.
